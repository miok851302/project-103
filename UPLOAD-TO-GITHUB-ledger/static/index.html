HOW TO READ THIS

Curated issues and filing hits answer different questions.

Curated issues are researched by hand: what was reported, how it was resolved or where it stands, with a link to the source. They are chosen to show how management has handled pressure.

Filing hits are automated keyword matches in official documents. A sentence mentioning “class action” can be a routine risk disclosure, not a live lawsuit against the company — so read the linked page before concluding.

Not investment advice. Ledger is a research aid. Curated records cover a limited set of companies and are reviewed periodically — they are not a complete list, and a company without a curated record is not a clean company.
